TikTok for Developers Integration

TudClip integrates with TikTok via the official TikTok Developer Platform (Login Kit and Content Posting API) to empower content creators with effortless video publishing:

Login Kit (user.info.basic) Enables creators to securely link their TikTok account via OAuth 2.0 and display their account username within TudClip.
Content Posting API (video.upload) Uploads video chunks directly to TikTok servers using the chunked upload protocol.
Content Posting API (video.publish) Finalizes and posts the creator's video to their TikTok feed with user-specified captions, custom covers, and privacy settings.
